Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • Effective for all orders on earthen8knot.com
Summary of Delivery Timelines:
• Artisanal Crafting & Processing: 3–5 business days
• Courier Transit: 5–7 business days
• Total Estimated Delivery Time: 8–14 business days (Average 10–12 days across India).

1. Slow Fashion & Made-to-Order Production

At Earthen8Knot, we celebrate mindful, sustainable slow craft. We do not mass-produce in factories. Each pillow, throw, top, and bag is individually hand-knitted and hand-stitched by our skilled artisans upon receiving your order. Please allow 3 to 5 business days for your piece to be crafted, inspected for quality, and packaged with eco-friendly jute and kraft materials.

2. Delivery Timelines

Once your order is crafted and passes our quality check, it is dispatched via reputed courier partners (Delhivery, Blue Dart, DTDC, or India Post Express). Transit time within India typically ranges between 5 to 7 business days depending on your location and pincode.

  • Metro Cities (Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi NCR, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata): 8–10 business days total.
  • Tier 2 & Tier 3 Cities / Rest of India: 10–14 business days total.

3. WhatsApp Quality Tour (Pre-Dispatch Preview)

We want you to adore your handmade item. If you select the "See my items before dispatch (WhatsApp Tour)" checkbox at checkout, our artisan coordinator will send you a photo or short video clip of your exact finished piece on WhatsApp before sealing the shipping box.

4. Order Tracking

As soon as your parcel is handed over to the courier partner, an automated shipping confirmation with a live tracking number and link is sent to your registered email and WhatsApp number. You can also view your live order status anytime at My Orders.

5. Shipping Charges

We are delighted to offer Complimentary Free Shipping across all serviceable pincodes in India for orders placed on our website.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • Earthen8Knot Slow Craft Policy
Important Note: Because each item is lovingly handcrafted specifically for you upon receiving your order, all orders are final (non-refundable & non-exchangeable) once production begins.

1. Order Cancellation Window

We understand plans can change. You may cancel your order within 12 hours of placing it on our website. To cancel, please email us immediately at earthen8knot@gmail.com with your Order Reference ID (e.g. #EK-XXXXXX).

Once the 12-hour window has elapsed, our artisans allocate yarns and begin handcrafting, and the order cannot be cancelled or refunded.

2. Handmade Craft Non-Refundable Policy

Each Earthen8Knot creation requires hours of dedicated hand labor. Slight variations in yarn shade, stitch tension, and weave texture are natural hallmarks of authentic handmade crochet and do not constitute flaws. Consequently, we do not accept returns or exchanges for change of mind or personal aesthetic preference.

3. Damaged in Transit or Defective Items Guarantee

While we inspect every item stringently and pack it securely in multi-layered protective wrap, in the unlikely event your product arrives damaged in transit or with an undeniable structural defect:

  • Notify us within 48 hours of delivery by emailing earthen8knot@gmail.com.
  • Include your Order Reference ID, a brief description of the issue, and clear unboxing photographs or a short video demonstrating the damage.
  • Upon verification by our support team, we will immediately handcraft and dispatch a Free Brand-New Replacement to your address at zero additional cost.

4. Approved Refunds

In any circumstance where an exceptional refund is approved by our management, the refunded amount will be processed automatically to your original payment method (Bank Account, Credit/Debit Card, or UPI) via Razorpay within 5 to 7 business days.
